## How learning works

1. Open the next packet under `_docs/guides/packets/`.
2. You do the learner steps (run, click, answer MCQ).
3. Cursor implements only what the packet allows.
4. Paste the packet into Gemini with `gemini-prompt-wrapper.md` → narration → save under `_docs/notes/`.
5. Stop after one packet; permission before the next.
